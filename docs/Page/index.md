<section class="section">
  <h2>One small app for the things macOS does not remember.</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">Use Sentrio to describe the result you want at a desk, in a meeting room or with a docking station. It handles the ordinary device churn around it.</p>
  <div class="feature-grid">
    <article class="feature"><h3>Choose the real priority</h3><p>Rank microphones and speakers once. Sentrio selects the best available device instead of leaving the decision to chance.</p></article>
    <article class="feature"><h3>Restore the right levels</h3><p>Keep separate output, input and alert volumes for each device. Switching hardware does not have to reset a working setup.</p></article>
    <article class="feature"><h3>Make presence visible</h3><p>Drive a BusyLight with rules, manual controls, local HTTP and macOS integrations that fit your day.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
