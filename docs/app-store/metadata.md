## English (U.S.)

**Name:** Sentrio Sound

**Subtitle:** Audio and BusyLight control

**Promotional text:** Keep audio routing and desk presence predictable as devices come and go.

**Keywords:** audio,headset,microphone,volume,BusyLight,menu bar

**Description:**

Sentrio remembers how your microphones, speakers and BusyLight should behave. Rank devices once, keep separate volume levels and use local rules to keep a desk or meeting room in the state you expect.

Sentrio works from the macOS menu bar. It keeps current routing legible, restores preferred audio devices when hardware changes and can make presence visible on a supported BusyLight.
